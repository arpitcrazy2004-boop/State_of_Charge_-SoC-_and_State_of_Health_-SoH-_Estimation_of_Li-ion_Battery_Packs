### About the Experiment 

This experiment focuses on estimating the State of Charge (SoC) and State of Health (SoH) of Lithium-ion (Li-ion) battery packs. It introduces the basic principles of battery monitoring, charge estimation, battery aging, and performance evaluation.
The experiment aims to help students understand how a Battery Management System (BMS) monitors battery conditions, estimates the remaining charge, evaluates battery health, and supports the safe and efficient operation of battery packs used in electric vehicles and energy storage systems.
